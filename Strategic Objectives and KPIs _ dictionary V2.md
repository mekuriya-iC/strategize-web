**The i-Capital Africa Institute**

**Strategy Cascading Dictionary**

**Corporate Strategic Objectives and KPIs: Definitions**

1.  **Increase i-Capital's shareholder value**

    1.  Total Revenue from OD, Capital Market, CBS (in million ETB)

    2.  Gross Profit in million

    3.  Total Cost of Sales to Revenue Ratio from core offerings

    4.  Total Cost of Sales to Revenue Ratio from non-core offerings

    5.  Return on Invested Capital (ROIC)

    6.  Percentage of total revenue from new service lines/products
        (launched in last 2 years)

2.  **Improve client and stakeholder experiences across all core
    operations**

    1.  Customer Satisfaction Index (CSI) (%)

    2.  Trust index (%)

    3.  Repeat purchase rate

    4.  Customer Conversion Rate

    5.  Net Promoter Score (NPS)

3.  **Diversify i-Capital business portfolio**

    1.  Number of Strategic Business Units (SBUs) emerged

    2.  Percentage of total revenue from new business areas or markets

    3.  Number of pilot or innovation projects launched or incubated
        annually

4.  **Improve project management**

    1.  Percentage of projects delivered on budget

    2.  Percentage of projects delivered on schedule

    3.  Proposal Success Rate of core offerings

5.  **Enhance delivery and quality of core services**

    1.  Proposal success rate of core offerings

    2.  Total number of projects successfully delivered from all
        category of core offerings

    3.  Number of government clients served with high value (\> 2Mil
        ETB) projects

6.  **Expand value added partnership**

    1.  Number of active international partners (engaged on at least 1
        project per year)

    2.  Number of active local partners (engaged on at least 1 project
        per year)

    3.  Number of joint solution/innovation projects delivered with
        partners

    4.  Number of joint proposals jointly produced through inter-unit
        collaboration within i-Capital

    5.  Number of knowledge-sharing or thought leadership collaborations
        with partners

7.  **Enhance efficiency and internal capacity of i-Capital**

    1.  No of operating system/policies devised /revised.

    2.  Number of staff participated in benchmarking or exposure visits

    3.  No employees received mentoring or training in accordance with
        gaps

    4.  Percentage of leadership and staff members who achieve the
        targeted set of skills

    5.  Satisfaction rate of internal staffs

    6.  Staff retention rate

    7.  Average training hours per employee per year

    8.  Number of IT/Digital platforms designed/implemented to improve
        internal processes

8.  **Strengthen Innovation, Knowledge Management, and Thought
    Leadership**

    1.  Number of pilot or innovation projects launched or incubated
        annually

    2.  Number of new knowledge products or publications produced

    3.  Number of industry events, workshops, or public forums led by
        i-Capital

**Description of Key Functions/Positions For the Purpose of KPI**

1.  **Corporate:** At i-Capital Africa Institute, **"Corporate"** refers
    to the central, organization-wide level of the company that sets
    strategy, direction, policies, and standards across all units and
    business functions. The corporate level is typically represented by
    the CEO, Board of Directors, and core strategic leadership team.
    This level has a "whole company" view---distinct from divisions,
    departments, or specific business units. **Corporate** is
    responsible for vision, mission, overall strategic objectives,
    governance, resource allocation, brand positioning, institutional
    risk management, and overall organizational performance. It is the
    "apex" of the strategy and performance cascade, with all other units
    aligning their objectives and results to support the corporate
    agenda.

2.  **Corporate KPIs** are high-level Key Performance Indicators that
    measure the achievement of i-Capital's top strategic objectives and
    overall organizational health. They are set and owned at the
    corporate level (CEO/Board), and provide a holistic assessment of
    how well the entire company is performing.

3.  **Chief Executive Officer:** The CEO is the highest-ranking
    executive in i-Capital Africa Institute and is ultimately
    responsible for the overall leadership, management, and strategic
    direction of the organization. All major functional heads and
    business units report directly to the CEO.

4.  **Operation Division (OD):** The Operations Division is the engine
    of i-Capital's service delivery, responsible for the end-to-end
    management and execution of the company's consulting, research,
    learning, and knowledge-sharing solutions. OD ensures that all
    client engagements are designed, delivered, and continuously
    improved to achieve strategic business objectives and deliver
    measurable value to clients and stakeholders. OD reports to the CEO
    and directly cascades its KPIs from related Corporate KPIs.

5.  **Capital Markets Services (CMS) Department:** CMS is a specialized
    business unit of i-Capital Africa Institute responsible for
    delivering advisory, transaction support, compliance guidance, and
    the origination and development of capital market products and
    services---including IPOs, bonds, direct listings, and innovative
    financial instruments---primarily for financial sector clients. CMS
    operates in alignment with the Ethiopian Capital Market Authority
    (ECMA) licensing framework, ensuring that all capital
    markets-related projects, investor education, and market development
    initiatives are executed to the highest professional and regulatory
    standards, and directly cascades its KPIs from i-Capital's corporate
    objectives.

6.  **Cross-Border Solutions (CBS):** Cross-Border Solutions (CBS) is a
    standalone business unit responsible for developing and managing
    i-Capital's international consulting, training, and partnership
    projects, leading market entry into new countries, building
    cross-border alliances, and exporting the institute's services and
    expertise to clients and partners across Africa and beyond, with a
    focus on innovative and adaptive delivery. The unit's Key
    Performance Indicators (KPIs) are directly cascaded from i-Capital's
    corporate-level objectives, ensuring that CBS's activities and
    outcomes are strategically aligned with the institute's overarching
    growth, impact, and market expansion goals.

7.  **Finance & Portfolio Management Department:** The Finance &
    Portfolio Management Department is responsible for the comprehensive
    oversight of i-Capital's financial management, administrative
    operations, human resources, and investment portfolio development.
    This department ensures effective budgeting and financial
    discipline, efficient and compliant administrative services, robust
    HR management---including recruitment, onboarding, performance
    management, payroll, staff well-being, and professional
    development---, asset management, and the identification and
    execution of strategic investments. Through these integrated
    functions, the department underpins i-Capital's sustainable growth,
    operational resilience, and proactive risk management.

8.  **Relationship & Growth Department:** Relationship & Growth is the
    business development and partnership arm of i-Capital Africa
    Institute, leading efforts in client acquisition, retention,
    marketing, brand management, and the expansion of local and
    international partnerships to drive the institute's market presence,
    revenue growth, and reputation as a trusted thought partner.

9.  **Strategy & MEL (Monitoring, Evaluation & Learning) Unit:** The
    Strategy & MEL Unit is responsible for steering the institute's
    strategic planning, institutional performance measurement,
    monitoring, evaluation, and organizational learning, providing
    leadership in KPI management, data-driven decision-making, and
    continuous improvement across all business units.

10. **Innovation Lead (Strategic Portfolios & Initiatives):** The
    Innovation Lead manages the development and implementation of new
    portfolios, innovation projects, and strategic business initiatives
    for i-Capital, guiding them from ideation and feasibility through
    pilot, launch, and successful integration into existing units or the
    creation of new business lines, ensuring all projects are aligned
    with the institute's mission and long-term strategy.

11. **Legal Unit:** The Legal Unit is an independent function that
    ensures i-Capital's full compliance with applicable laws and
    regulations, manages contract negotiations and legal risk, oversees
    corporate governance matters, and provides timely legal counsel to
    all business units to safeguard the company's interests and
    reputation.

12. **Learning Solutions (LS) Department:** The Learning Solutions
    Department is responsible for designing, delivering, and evaluating
    customized and standardized training programs, workshops, and
    capacity-building initiatives that address client and sector needs,
    utilizing innovative learning methodologies and technologies to
    build individual and institutional capabilities and support
    i-Capital's strategic objectives. The department's Key Performance
    Indicators (KPIs) are cascaded from and aligned with those of the
    Operations Division, ensuring that its performance directly
    contributes to the core service delivery and business goals of
    i-Capital.

13. **Consulting & Research Services (CRS) Department:** The Consulting
    & Research Services (CRS) Department is responsible for delivering
    expert advisory, consulting, and applied research
    solutions---including organizational assessments, strategic
    planning, feasibility studies, transaction advisory, and policy
    analysis---tailored to the needs of clients in the public, private,
    and development sectors. The department's Key Performance Indicators
    (KPIs) are cascaded from and aligned with those of the Operations
    Division, ensuring that CRS's performance directly supports the
    division's and i-Capital's core business objectives, quality
    standards, and impact targets.

14. **Knowledge Sharing Platform (KSP) Coordinator:** The Knowledge
    Sharing Platform (KSP) function is responsible for developing,
    managing, and facilitating in-person and digital platforms---such as
    forums, summits, workshops, and online communities---that enable
    sector-wide knowledge exchange, peer learning, and thought
    leadership. This includes the planning and organization of flagship
    events like the East Africa Finance Summit, as well as the
    documentation, dissemination, and follow-up of proceedings and
    knowledge products to maximize sector impact. The function's Key
    Performance Indicators (KPIs) are cascaded from and aligned with
    those of the Operations Division, ensuring that KSP's activities
    advance i-Capital's strategic objectives for learning, sector
    development, and stakeholder engagement.

15. **Chief Compliance Officer (CCO):** The Chief Compliance Officer
    (CCO) reports to the Manager of the Capital Market Services (CMS)
    Department and is responsible for executing all appointed
    representative compliance functions as mandated by the Ethiopian
    Capital Market Authority (ECMA) directives. The CCO ensures that all
    advisory and transaction activities strictly adhere to regulatory
    requirements, manages internal compliance monitoring systems,
    supports regulatory filings and audits, and fulfills additional Key
    Performance Indicators (KPIs) assigned by the CMS Manager to support
    departmental and institutional compliance objectives.

21. **Junior Consultant:** A Junior Consultant is an entry-level
    professional who reports to either the Learning Solutions (LS) or
    Consulting & Research Services (CRS) Department, depending on their
    placement. Junior Consultants support the design, delivery, and
    evaluation of consulting, training, or research projects by
    conducting background research, assisting with content development,
    supporting client engagements, and performing assigned tasks that
    contribute to the achievement of departmental Key Performance
    Indicators (KPIs) and project outcomes.

    objectives and KPIs are met. Consultants contribute technical
    expertise, project management skills, and innovative problem-solving
    to support departmental and organizational goals.

    consulting, training, or research outputs, and drive achievement of
    department and division KPIs. They also contribute to business
    development, thought leadership, and the continuous improvement of
    service offerings.

27. **Shared KPIs** (Key Performance Indicators) are performance
    measures that are jointly owned and influenced by two or more
    departments, teams, or individuals across the organization. Rather
    than being limited to a single unit, these KPIs reflect outcomes or
    processes that require collaboration and alignment among multiple
    functions, driving collective responsibility and a "one company"
    mindset.
